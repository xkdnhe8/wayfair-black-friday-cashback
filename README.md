## FAQ

**When do Wayfair Black Friday doorbusters start?**
Midnight ET on the sale's opening day. In 2025 that was 12 a.m. ET on November 28, with early deals running from November 20 and member/App early access before that. Expect the same structure in late November, and expect limited quantities.

**Is Wayfair Rewards the same thing as the Wayfair credit card?**
No — a point of frequent confusion. Wayfair Rewards is a $29/year shopping membership with 5% Reward Dollars on qualifying purchases and no-minimum free shipping. The Wayfair credit card is a Citibank-issued Mastercard that earns 7% at Wayfair brands and includes a free Rewards membership while the card is open. **Can I stack cash back with Wayfair Rewards or the credit card?**
No — you earn through one channel per order. If a purchase earns 5% Reward Dollars through Wayfair Rewards or 7% through the card, it can't also earn TopCashback cash back, and vice versa.

**Is Way Day the same thing as Black Friday at Wayfair?**
It's the same playbook at a different date. Way Day runs twice a year (spring and fall) with up to 80% off, midnight doorbusters, and sitewide free shipping; the fall edition lands about a month before Black Friday. If you miss a doorbuster during Way Day, the Black Friday season is your next realistic shot at comparable pricing.

**Does free shipping cover everything?**
During the main events, shipping is free sitewide — except Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, and non-standard items like flooring and certain oversized fixtures, which may carry charges. Wayfair Rewards members' free shipping has the same geographic exclusions.

**How long until my cash back is payable?**
